## Ship-To Fields

- `shipToName` - Contact person at delivery site
- `shipToAddress` - Delivery address
- `shipToPhone` - Contact phone
- `shipToContact` - Additional contact

## Payment Recording

When marking invoice as PAID:
- Required: `paymentMethod`, `paymentRef`, `paidAt`
- Updates complaint status to PAID (if linked)
- Triggers PAID → CLOSED transition (automatic)
